Quick answer

Choose an authorized supplier that documents reseller eligibility, approved staff access, recovery and role changes, order and credit records, the complete price and practical support. Ask for an approved demonstration of a normal order and a documented exception. Use an eligible consumer trial only for the viewing questions it can answer. Wait on a commercial commitment when authority, the credit impact or the next support action is unclear.

Define which operations the business actually needs

List the approved staff members and the tasks they are expected to perform. One person may need to review a customer case, another to compare credits with an invoice. An owner may be responsible for changing staff permissions. Ask the supplier to identify the role for each task and which terms support it. That list helps you avoid paying for an administration system designed for a different operation. It also makes it possible to compare two proposals without granting unnecessary access simply because the original description sounded convenient.
